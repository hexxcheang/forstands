    The sample board

    Two-sided, on a triangular base

    A 30 mm board, 1,200 mm tall overall, slotted into a triangular base that braces it in front and behind. The base is 450 mm front to back and 220 mm high, as long as the board is wide. Samples are fixed to both faces, so the board works from either side of the booth. In the planner it comes 600, 800, 1,000 or 1,200 mm wide, with Lusano switches, Lenovo locks, or Maxsen smart home and the S8 panel on its faces.

    • Board: 30 mm MDF or ply, laminated both faces, with a brand header printed or routed at the top and a capping strip along the top edge.
    • Base: a hollow triangular prism in metal or veneered ply, the board seated in a slot at its apex. Weight it inside, for example with a steel plate, so a 1.2 m board can't be tipped by a visitor.
    • Samples: real switch plates or locks screwed through from behind, with the wiring for any live samples run down inside the board to the base.
    • Four boards at 600 wide each take about 0.27 m² of floor; set them square to the aisle so both faces are seen, or turn them to guide visitors in.
