A 1.4 m counter, 850 mm high, with a white marble top under a thin, flat gold top: Lusano Swarovski crystal switches and the Halo dimmer along its front edge, six Lenovo smart locks on top, each on its own stand and staggered in two rows, and a ball light at the front left that the Halo dimmer and the switches control. Below, a light wood body with LUSANO in bronze letters, lit from behind, on its closed centre, and identical open shelves curving round both front corners, with catalogues on the top shelves and trailing plants spilling over the middle ones. Addressable RGBCCT LED strips run under the top, under each shelf and under the base.

    Build notes

    What the fabricator needs to know

    Top

    • Marble: 1,400 × 500, 150 mm corner radius, 120 mm thick with a crisp, slightly eased edge.
    • Gold top: 1,540 × 550, 165 mm corner radius, 10 mm thick, following the marble's outline at 1.1×. Brushed gold PVD stainless steel, about 1.2 mm, wrapped over an 8 mm board, bonded to the marble. It overhangs 70 mm at each end and 25 mm front and back, so it shades the switch row slightly. Overall height becomes 1,010 mm.
    • White marble, or marble-look sintered stone, 12 mm on a plywood core with a built-up edge, so it stays liftable. Real marble needs sealing against stains.
    • Seven cut-outs on the front edge, 125 mm apart and centred 60 mm below the top surface: three Lusano Noble at the left, the Halo dimmer in the middle, and three Lusano Prestige at the right, all 86-type. Back boxes sit inside the top's core; wiring runs down through the centre.
    • Six lock displays, each a separate stand, staggered in two rows so each lock is seen past its neighbours: a 150 × 550 mm door panel, 45 mm thick like a real door, on its own 170 × 150 mm bronze foot. Each carries a real Lenovo lock fitted as on a door: the outside body on the front, the inside unit with its thumb-turn on the back, and the mortise faceplate and latch on the edge. Bolt the feet through the top so they can't be knocked over.
    • Power the locks from their own batteries as normal, so the keypads, cameras and fingerprint readers all work for visitors. Lock sizes are measured from the product photos; confirm them against MyDigitalLock's spec sheet before cutting the panels.
    • Ball light: a 200 mm opal glass globe on a bronze base at the front left corner of the top, with a tunable-white smart lamp inside, paired to the gateway. Halo sets its brightness and warmth; the Lusano keys switch it and call scenes.

    Body

    • Plywood with a light wood veneer, such as white oak or ash, outside and inside the bays.
    • Both end bays are identical and open: shelves at 307 and 513, evenly spaced, that curve round the front corner, with only the back half of each end closed. A4 catalogues on the top shelves.
    • Trailing plants on the middle shelves, in 100–120 mm pots set near the front edge so the vines fall clear of the shelves below. For a show of a few days real plants are fine; for longer, use good artificial trailing plants, since the bay LEDs won't keep real ones healthy. Keep vines clear of the base LED line.
    • Centre: closed with a removable light wood panel, 660 wide. LUSANO in bronze-finish letters, 560 mm wide with letters about 100 mm tall, stood 15 mm off the panel with a warm LED behind them, so they glow at the edges (halo lighting).

    Light

    • IC RGBCCT LED strip throughout: RGB plus warm and cool white chips, with driver ICs so each pixel or segment can be set on its own.
    • Under the top edge all the way round, under the front edge of each shelf, and under the base, in aluminium channels with opal diffusers so the pixels blend into a line.
    • Run them from an addressable (SPI) controller linked to the Maxsen gateway: warm white for the show, and colour scenes or flowing effects for launches and photos.
    • LUSANO is lit separately in warm white, so it stays on brand whatever the strips are doing.

    Power and height

    • One 13 A point, entering through the base. The 24 V power supply, the SPI controller and the gateway sit behind the centre panel.
    • 850 mm to the top of the gold: the 120 mm marble top sits on a 720 mm body, and the gold adds 10 mm. That is worktop height, easy for anyone to reach the switches and the Halo dimmer. It also puts each lock's keypad at about 1.15 m, close to where it sits on a real door. The tallest point is the lock stands, at about 1.41 m. The body fills the space below, so there is no knee room for sitting.
    To confirm

    Four things to check

    DepthNow 500 mm, as you asked. The lock feet take 160 mm of it and sit just behind the middle, leaving room to lean on the front.
    Gangs and locksThe Lusano switches are shown as 1-gang faces in each finish; you can mix in 2- and 3-gang. The three locks are F3 Ultra; a gate lock can replace one.
    Panel widthThe closed centre is 660 mm wide, which keeps the shelf bays usable either side. Widening it to about 1 m would leave the end bays too narrow for shelves on a 1.4 m counter.
    Where it goes in the boothIt could replace the two round tables as one consult counter, or sit beside them. Tell me and I'll add it to the booth plan.
