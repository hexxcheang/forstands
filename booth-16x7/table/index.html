Build notes

What the fabricator needs to know

Top

  • 1,400 × 600, 150 mm corner radius, 120 mm thick with a crisp, slightly eased edge.
  • Porcelain marble-look sintered stone, 12 mm on a plywood core with a built-up edge, so it stays liftable.
  • Seven cut-outs on the front edge, 125 mm apart and centred 60 mm below the top surface: six 86-type smart switches with the Halo dimmer in the middle. Back boxes sit inside the top's core; wiring runs down through the centre.
  • Three lock displays on top: a 220 × 460 mm door panel on a bronze foot, each with a Lenovo smart lock fitted as on a real door. Bolt the feet through the top so they can't be knocked over.

Body

  • Plywood with Midnight Bronze wood veneer outside and champagne veneer inside the bays and niche.
  • Left bay: two straight shelves. Right bay: two shelves that curve round the front corner. Shelves at 330 and 600.
  • Centre: closed with a removable Midnight Bronze panel, 660 wide. The Maxsen monogram and wordmark are cut through and backed with opal acrylic, lit from behind, so the logo glows.

Light

  • IC RGBCCT LED strip throughout: RGB plus warm and cool white chips, with driver ICs so each pixel or segment can be set on its own.
  • Under the top edge all the way round, under the front edge of each shelf, and under the base, in aluminium channels with opal diffusers so the pixels blend into a line.
  • Run them from an addressable (SPI) controller linked to the Maxsen gateway: warm white for the show, and colour scenes or flowing effects for launches and photos.
  • The logo is lit separately in white, so it stays on brand whatever the strips are doing.

Power and height

  • One 13 A point, entering through the base. The 24 V power supply, the SPI controller and the gateway sit behind the centre panel.
  • 1,000 mm is right for a standing counter: the body fills the space below, so there is no knee room for sitting.
To confirm

Four things to check

DepthStill assumed at 600 mm. The locks need about 160 mm of depth each; anything from 500 mm works.
Which switches and locksShown: Lusano Prestige, Lusano Noble, two Nova+ Pro, Folio and Ark, with Halo in the centre; and three F3 Ultra locks. Swap in any you prefer, or a gate lock.
Panel widthThe closed centre is 660 mm wide, which keeps the shelf bays usable either side. Widening it to about 1 m would leave the end bays too narrow for shelves on a 1.4 m counter.
Where it goes in the boothIt could replace the two round tables as one consult counter, or sit beside them. Tell me and I'll add it to the booth plan.
