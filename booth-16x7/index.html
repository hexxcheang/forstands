The big L

Carpentry wall and fabric lightbox

The carpentry wall is one-sided: the products face into the booth and the back faces the neighbour. It is built as four panels of about 1,190 mm so it breaks down for the next show. The lightbox gives the booth its glow and carries the brand line.

Fabric lightbox

  • 2,134 × 1,800 × 120 mm aluminium frame along the full 7 ft side. About 1,980 mm of lit face shows beside the carpentry wall.
  • Printed fabric with a silicone edge (SEG) pushed into the frame, lit from behind by LED modules. A new graphic is a new print; the frame stays.
  • Artwork: the Maxsen wordmark, "The art of a single touch." and the Halo ring.

Carpentry wall

  • Four panels, about 1,190 × 1,800 × 150 mm, plywood on a timber frame with a champagne laminate face. They bolt to each other and to the lightbox frame, which keeps the back L standing.
  • A slim bronze LED light channel runs across each panel just above the cassette and washes the products with warm light. Nothing overhangs the visitors.
  • Each panel takes an 800 × 500 mm product cassette that lifts out for transport.
  • Service hatch behind each lower panel for wiring, power strip, gateway and router.
The corner stand

One two-sided stand, angled across the front corner

One 1,000 mm stand at 45° does the work of two: its aisle face looks out at the corner, so people in both aisles see it, and its inside face looks back at the tables. It stands on its own, so it gets a wider 600 mm steel base, set fully inside the booth line. There is no canopy.

  1. Header, 1,400–1,800Product name in the Maxsen serif, to the top of the stand. Printed fabric (SEG) skin.
  2. Light channel, 1,360–1,390A 30 mm bronze channel with Lumi LED strip, standing 30 mm off the face. It lights the cassette below, so the stand needs no canopy.
  3. Product cassette, 850–1,350800 × 500 board, 18 mm ply with a laminate face, cut for 86-type back boxes. It lifts out with four thumb screws.
  4. Info strip, 550–850Finishes, a line of specs and a QR to pricing and packages.
  5. Lower skin, 50–550The Maxsen lattice on fabric. It zips off to reach the power strip and cables.

Lighting

Lit by your own products, with no roof

There are no canopies, so the product lighting sits in the faces themselves: six LED light channels with Lumi LED strip, one above each cassette. The lightbox lights the seating. Programme these scenes on the gateway and put them on the S8 and S10 panels.

Power and data

Three power points and one network

All 230 V wiring by a licensed electrical worker, on RCD-protected strips inside the hatches. Cables run behind the wall and under a flat floor cover along the side edge to the corner stand, never across the centre path.

Visitor journey

From the aisle to a booked visit

  1. NoticeThe lit screens on the corner stand catch both aisles at once. The lightbox glows behind.
  2. TouchOn the product wall, visitors press a Lusano key and the light above it answers. They turn Halo and watch C2 warm up.
  3. SitA consultant takes them to a table, opens the Smart Life app on the iPad and picks finishes from the tray.
  4. Leave with a planThe package menus stand on the tables, and the smart lock face looks back at them. Visitors scan the QR or save the WhatsApp number to book a showroom visit or a site check.

Two people on the booth

  • Host at the corner stand: greets from both aisles, demos the panels and the locks, brings people in.
  • Consultant at the tables: walks the product wall with visitors, then sits down to price a package.
  • At busy times, a third person stays on the product wall.

Keep on hand

  • Spare switches for each finish on the wall, in case one is knocked.
  • A charged power bank for each iPad.
  • Microfibre cloths: crystal and glass faces show fingerprints.

Before fabrication

Four things to confirm

Your digital lock standsThere's still no room for a separate lock stand. Either one of yours becomes the corner stand, or a live lock goes on its inside face. Tell me which, and the size of your stands.
Which corner is openDrawn with aisles at the front and on the right. If the open side is the left, mirror the plan.
PowerThree 13 A points: lightbox, carpentry wall and the corner stand. Book them with the organiser once the fabricator confirms the load.
Offers at the eventThe site lists a free S8 with qualifying packages at the showroom, and a free S1 with any F3 Ultra and a Premium Core package. Confirm both apply at the booth before the package cards are printed.
