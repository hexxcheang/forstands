Layout

Top view

Drawn for a corner booth with a neighbour at the back and on the left, and aisles at the front and on the right. The lightbox and the carpentry wall meet in the back corner. The two stands bolt together into an L in the front right corner, diagonally opposite, so the booth reads as two L shapes facing each other.

The 800 mm path between the two tables leads straight from the front aisle to the product wall. Visitors also come in from the side aisle, above the small L, and walk along the 900 mm strip in front of the carpentry wall.

The big L

Carpentry wall and fabric lightbox

The carpentry wall is one-sided: the products face into the booth and the back faces the neighbour. It is built as four panels of about 1,190 mm so it breaks down and goes back up at the next show. The lightbox gives the booth its glow and carries the brand line.

Fabric lightbox

  • 2,134 × 2,400 × 120 mm aluminium frame, the full 7 ft side. About 1,980 mm of lit face shows beside the carpentry wall.
  • Printed fabric with a silicone edge (SEG) pushed into the frame. A new graphic is a new print; the frame and LED backlight stay.
  • Lit from behind by LED modules. One 13 A point.
  • Suggested artwork: the Maxsen wordmark and "The art of a single touch." from your Halo campaign.

Carpentry wall

  • Four panels, about 1,190 × 2,400 × 150 mm, plywood on a timber frame with a laminate face. They bolt together and to the lightbox frame.
  • A 300 mm fascia along the top holds two Luna downlights per panel, lighting the products below.
  • Each panel takes the same 800 × 700 mm product cassette as the stands, so switches lift out for transport.
  • Service hatch behind the lower fabric panel for wiring, power strip and gateway.
The small L

Two two-sided stands at the front corner

S1 runs along the front aisle and S2 along the side aisle. Bolted at the corner, the L braces itself, so each stand needs only a 400 mm base and nothing sticks out past the booth line. Outside faces pull people in from both aisles. Inside faces are what visitors see from the tables.

Five things to confirm

Your digital lock standsThere's no room for separate lock stands in this layout. Either they become S1 and S2 in the small L, or a live lock goes on the S2 outside face. Tell me which, and the size of your stands.
Which corner is openDrawn with aisles at the front and on the right. If the open side is the left, mirror the plan.
Height limitEverything is 2.4 m. Check the organiser's manual; some malls cap booths lower, especially for back walls.
PowerThree 13 A points: lightbox, carpentry wall and the small L. All 230 V wiring by a licensed electrical worker, on RCD-protected strips.
Offers at the eventThe site lists a free S8 with qualifying packages at the showroom, and a free S1 with any F3 Ultra and a Premium Core package. Confirm both apply at the booth before the package cards are printed.
